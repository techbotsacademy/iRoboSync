Create a simple low-poly 3D model of a basic passenger aeroplane.

The model should have:

A cylindrical fuselage with a rounded nose
Two straight wings, one on each side
Two simple jet engines mounted under the wings
A vertical tail fin
Two small horizontal stabilizers at the rear
Simple cockpit windows
No interior
No landing gear
No complex details or textures

Use clean, smooth geometry with simple shapes and proportions. Make the model suitable for beginners to 3D print, modify, and use in a classroom project. Keep the number of polygons low and avoid thin or fragile parts.
