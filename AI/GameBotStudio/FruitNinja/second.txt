Create a Fruit Ninja game using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Requirements:



Full-screen canvas.

Use fruits - Watermelon, Mango, Pineapple, Strawberry, Kiwi, Bananas, and Apples.

Fruits follow a realistic arc.

Detect collisions between the cursor and fruits.

Increase the score when a fruit is sliced.

Show an animation when fruits are cut.

End the game after three missed fruits. 

Use AI to capture the hand movements and use the finger tip as the input to slice the fruit
