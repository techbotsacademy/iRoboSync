1. Overall Camera & World Perspective
Camera Angle: A true pseudo-3D third-person camera positioned slightly above and behind the player character, looking down the tracks toward a single horizon point ($Y \approx 240$ on a $400 \times 600$ canvas).
Forward Velocity: To create an intense feeling of forward movement, the tracks must feature dynamically scaling, moving ground sleepers (railroad ties) and passing side-scenery pillars that expand as they travel from the horizon ($z = 0$) to the foreground ($z = 1$).
2. Player Character (Back Pose & Active Running Animation)
During gameplay, the player must be viewed from the back, moving dynamically with a realistic, high-quality cartoon running cycle rather than looking like a static "gliding" figure.

Head & Hood: A large, solid, light-grey circle/oval (#E5E8EB) represents the fabric hood framing the back of the head. Underneath, a few dark brown hair spikes peek out at the nape of the neck. The back of the orange cap dome is visible with a small white plastic adjustment strap at the base.

🏃 Dynamic Run-Cycle Animation Mechanics
To replace the static "gliding" movement, animate the body parts using a time-based sinusoidal ($sin$) cycle:
Legs & Feet: Alternately lift, swing, and drop. One shoe should compress and rise towards the horizon while the other plant-steps lower down, shifting vertically and horizontally relative to the stride.
Shoulders & Arms: Swing out of phase with the legs. When the left leg moves forward, the right arm swings back, and vice-versa.
Torso Bounce & Sway: The entire character model must gently bob up and down (Y-axis bounce) and sway side-to-side (X-axis tilt) in sync with the stride cycle.
3. Realistic 3D Subway Trains (Extruded Depth)
To prevent trains from looking like flat, 2D planes that lose their 3D illusion as they approach, they must be rendered as fully extruded 3D boxes.

Continuous Projection Matrix: Define each train with a Front Z and a Back Z coordinate (e.g., Back Z is offset behind Front Z by a length of $-0.65$). Both coordinates must project through the perspective matrix.
Visible Faces:
Front Face Cabin: A polished silver and yellow rounded-rectangle panel featuring a sky-blue windshield and glowing white 3D headlamps.
Top Roof Plane: A trapezoid connecting the top of the Front Face to the top of the Back Face. Filled with a light grey metallic highlight (#8E9EAB).
Side Depth Face: A side panel connecting the side edges of the Front Face to the Back Face.
Perspective Logic: If a train is in the Left Lane, its right side wall is highly visible to the camera. If in the Right Lane, its left side wall is visible.
The side wall must feature moving yellow panel stripes and dark metallic window cuts.
No Disappearing Depth: As the train approaches the foreground ($z \rightarrow 1$), the distance between the Front Z and Back Z must scale proportionally. The back plane must stay projected in the distance, preserving a highly-visible 3D depth side profile until the train completely exits the screen.
4. Terrain & Elevation (Ramps & Platforms)
Elevated Platforms: Spawn concrete or metallic walkways above specific lanes (usually $120\text{px}$ higher than ground tracks).
Natural Transition Ramps: Connect ground level to these high platforms using smooth inclined ramps.
Player Physics Alignment: When the player switches to a lane containing a platform, they must smoothly run up the ramp, adapting their visual baseline height ($Y$) to the elevation curve of the platform.
Bonus Coins: Place gold coins suspended directly above the elevated platforms, rewarding players for climbing high.
5. Environment & Atmospheric Polish
Layered Scenery: The distant horizon must fade into dark gradients, while passing concrete structural arches scale up and slide past the side walls of the screen.
Cast Shadows: Draw translucent black elliptical shadows directly beneath the player, coins, and obstacles. The player's shadow must shrink and grow fainter as they jump higher into the air.
Glow & Lighting Effects: Add neon light trails and illuminated hazard lights on top of construction barriers.
