## Rules the evaluating AI must follow

- Score only what can be justified from the source code.
- Distinguish code-level design quality from measured runtime performance.
- Do not claim actual step-count accuracy, display latency, sampling stability, or battery life without test data.
- Cite specific variables, functions, statements, and missing safeguards as evidence.
- Explain each rating and provide actionable recommendations.
- Do not penalize the code merely for omitting features outside the stated project scope.
- Report critical defects separately from the numerical score.
