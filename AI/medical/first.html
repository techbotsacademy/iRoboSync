How it works
  1. The webcam captures your face.
  2. A region of skin on the forehead is selected.
  3. Small changes in reflected light caused by blood-volume changes are extracted from the video.
  4. The resulting signal is filtered to isolate the pulse component.
  5. Peak spacing is used to estimate heart rate.
  6. A much slower component is analyzed for an experimental breathing-rate estimate.
⚠️ This is an experimental computer-vision demonstration. It is NOT a medical device and should not be used to diagnose, monitor, or make treatment decisions.
