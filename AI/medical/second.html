Respiration / rPPG Signal

Improved respiration algorithm
  1. The webcam continuously samples the face and upper torso.
  2. The face signal uses chrominance-normalized RGB rather than the raw green channel.
  3. The upper-body region is analyzed for very slow periodic motion.
  4. The respiratory signal is restricted to approximately 6–30 breaths/minute.
  5. FFT identifies the strongest respiratory frequency.
  6. Autocorrelation independently estimates the breathing period.
  7. The two estimates are compared before accepting a result.
  8. Several recent windows are combined to stabilize the displayed value.
⚠️ Experimental only. This system is not a medical device. Camera-based vital-sign estimation can be affected by lighting, camera exposure, movement, clothing, camera quality, skin appearance, posture and many other factors. Do not use the displayed values for diagnosis or treatment.
